The Guru-Shishya Legacy

Honoring Our Masters with Rhythm & Devotion

GuruVandan is TablaGyan’s signature annual celebration — a sacred evening where every student, from beginners to seniors, presents a piece they have carefully prepared. After the students finish, our Gurus and invited guest artists take the stage. It is both a performance and a heartfelt offering of gratitude for the knowledge passed down through generations.

WHAT YOU WILL HEAR

Students first, then the masters

Student Presentations

Every learner — whether they have been with us for six months or six years — presents a short piece: a kayda, a rela, a tukda, or a vocal bandish. This is their moment to show what they have absorbed under their Guru’s guidance.

Guru & Guest Artists

Once the students have offered their music, the Gurus and invited maestros take the stage. Expect deep peshkars, intricate jugalbandis with sarangi or harmonium, and the rare joy of hearing multiple gharanas in one evening.
