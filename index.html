The Guru-Shishya Legacy

Honoring Our Masters with Rhythm & Devotion

GuruVandana is TablaGyan’s signature annual celebration — a sacred evening where every student, from beginners to seniors, presents a piece they have carefully prepared. After the students finish, our Gurus and invited guest artists take the stage. It is both a performance and a heartfelt offering of gratitude for the knowledge passed down through generations.

WHAT YOU WILL HEAR

Students first, then the masters

Student Presentations

Every learner — whether they have been with us for six months or six years — presents a short piece: a kayda, rela or tukda on the tabla, or a bandish or composition in vocals. This is their moment to show what they have absorbed under their Guru’s guidance.

Guru & Guest Artists

Once the students have offered their music, the Gurus and invited maestros take the stage. Expect deep peshkars and soulful vocal renditions, intricate jugalbandis with sarangi or harmonium, and the rare joy of hearing tabla and vocal traditions side by side in one evening.
